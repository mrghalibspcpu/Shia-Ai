## English

**ShiaVerse has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics.** It does not send
the books you read, your searches, your bookmarks, your notes or your
supplications anywhere.

- **Library data.** The Quran, Nahj al-Balagha, Mafatih al-Jinan, the Sahifa,
  the fourteen hadith books, the fatawa and the encyclopaedia are stored on your
  device. Books that are not bundled with the APK are downloaded from the
  project's public GitHub releases (`github.com/mrghalibspcpu/Shia-Ai`) and
  verified against a SHA-256 manifest before they are used.
- **Location.** Used only to compute prayer times and the Qibla bearing. The
  coordinate stays on the device. Naming a place may ask the public
  OpenStreetMap/Nominatim or BigDataCloud reverse-geocoding service, which
  receives the coordinate and nothing else; if you never press the location
  button, no request is made.
- **Map tiles.** The Qibla street map requests tiles from CARTO, an OpenStreetMap
  mirror or Esri. Those providers receive the tile coordinate, as every map does.
- **Notifications.** Prayer alerts are scheduled locally by the operating
  system. There is no push server.
- **Files on the device.** Bookmarks, notes, last-read markers, reader settings,
  tasbeeh counts and the daily-study plan are kept in the app's own preferences
  database. Uninstalling the app removes them.
- **Children.** The app collects nothing from anyone, of any age.
- **Changes.** Any change to this policy is published in this file in the same
  repository, with the version it applies to.
