1. Short version

  • No account, sign-in, advertising, in-app purchases, or analytics/crash-reporting SDK.
  • The app does not upload your bookmarks, notes, reading history, tasbeeh counts or settings to any server. They stay on your device.
  • The app does make network requests to third-party services in the situations listed in section 4. Those services necessarily receive your IP address and, in some cases, coordinates or a search text. We do not run a server of our own and do not receive that data.
  • ShiaVerse needs an internet connection to download its book databases (they are not inside the APK) and for the features in section 4. After a database is downloaded, reading and searching it works without a connection.

3. Permissions the app requests

PermissionWhy
INTERNETDownloading book databases and the services in section 4.
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION (optional)To compute prayer times and the Qibla bearing for your position. Computation happens on the device. You can refuse and pick a city manually, but if no GPS fix is available the app falls back to an IP-based lookup (section 4).
POST_NOTIFICATIONS, SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, VIBRATELocal prayer-time and calendar reminders, re-scheduled after a reboot, with sound/vibration. These are on-device notifications; no push service (such as Firebase) is used.

The compass uses the phone's magnetometer; the heading is processed on the device and never sent anywhere. The app does not request access to contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files, SMS or advertising ID.

These requests are made directly from your phone to the service named. Each service sees at least your IP address and normal HTTPS request data (such as the app's user-agent), and handles it under its own policy.

The app contains no advertising network, no analytics or crash-reporting library, and no social-media SDK.

5. Sharing, selling, retention

We do not sell or share personal data, and we operate no server that receives your data. Third-party services in section 4 may log requests according to their own policies; we have no control over their retention. Data on your device remains until you delete it, clear app storage or uninstall.

6. Children

ShiaVerse is a religious reference app, is not directed at children under 13, and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

7. Your choices

  • Deny or revoke the location permission in Android settings and choose a city manually (note the IP fallback above when no fix exists).
  • Deny the notification permission to stop reminders.
  • Do not download books, and avoid the Qibla map, if you do not want those requests made.
  • Delete everything: Android Settings → Apps → ShiaVerse → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall.

8. Religious content notice

Texts, translations and rulings shown in the app come from the sources listed in the app's documentation. They are provided for reading and reference; rulings (fatawa) should be verified at the original source linked in the app or with a qualified scholar.

9. Changes

If data handling changes, this page and its effective date will be updated before the new version is published.
