## Full description (English)

    ShiaVerse is a complete offline Shia study library for your phone. After a
    one-time download, the books live on your device — no account, no ads, no
    tracking, and reading works without internet.

    BOOKS
    • The Holy Quran with Urdu translation and tafseer notes, surah/juz/page
      navigation, and an optional continuous Mushaf mode (Arabic-only, justified
      pages) for traditional reading.
    • Mafatih al-Jinan with its supplications and acts, and Sahifa Sajjadiyya
      with all 83 supplications.
    • Nahj al-Balagha — sermons (khutbat), letters and wisdom, with footnotes.
    • Fourteen hadith books (nearly 100,000 narrations): al-Kafi, Man La
      Yahduruhu al-Faqih, Tahdhib al-Ahkam, al-Istibsar, Wasa'il al-Shia,
      Bihar-style collections, Mizan al-Hikmah, al-Ghurar wal-Hikam and more —
      each with real book titles and full chapter/section structure.
    • Religious rulings (fatawa) in Urdu from two maraji' — Ayatollah Sistani
      and Ayatollah Khamenei — every answer ends with the official source page
      and an "open the original" button so the ruling can be verified at the
      source.

    EVERYTHING WORKS OFFLINE
    • Full-text search across every installed book, with the index built on
      your own device.
    • Bookmarks, personal notes, and the last-read position in every book.
    • Reader themes, Arabic/Urdu text options, and adjustable font size.

    DAILY TOOLS
    • Prayer times for Shia (Jafari) calculation — with the Tehran method as an
      option — computed locally for your exact place, with the Hijri date
      (adjustable) and a full prayer-times screen.
    • Qibla compass: the direction of the Kaaba from your coordinates, with a
      three-dimensional dial, bearing, compass point and distance — works even
      on phones without a magnetometer.
    • Digital tasbeeh: Tasbeeh-e-Zahra (a.s.) with the 34-33-33 sequence,
      salawat, istighfar, tawheed and custom counters with your own wirds.
      Counts survive restarts, with daily and lifetime totals.

    YOUR DATA IS YOURS
    • No accounts, no analytics, no ads. Location is used only to calculate
      prayer times and Qibla on your device; you can always pick a city
      manually instead.
    • Book databases are verified (SHA-256) before they are opened.
    • A backup/restore feature exports your bookmarks, notes, positions and
      settings to a single file you control.

    Languages: Urdu interface with Arabic source texts.

## Permissions declaration (paste where the form asks)

* **Location (approximate & precise)** — used only on the device to compute
  Shia prayer times and the Qibla direction. Asked at runtime; if refused, the
  app works with a manually chosen city. Not sent to the developer or anyone
  else (place-name lookup services receive coordinates only when a place name
  is displayed).
* **Internet** — optional: downloading the book databases the user requests
  (verified by SHA-256), IP-based location fallback when GPS is unavailable,
  place-name lookups, and opening official source pages the user taps. No
  analytics, no ads, no data collection.
